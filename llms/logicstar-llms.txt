# LogicStar

> LogicStar is an AI platform for autonomous software maintenance. Its AI agent investigates, reproduces, and fixes real bugs from a team's backlog — reproducing each bug with a failing test that proves it is real, then validating that the proposed fix resolves it before opening a pull request. Founded in 2024 in Zurich by the team behind DeepCode.ai (acquired by Snyk / Snyk Code), spun out of INSAIT/ETH Zurich, and backed by Northzone.

Note: LogicStar is a product platform (used via app.logicstar.ai and Git/CI integrations). As of this writing it publishes no public developer API, SDK, or OpenAPI specification. Integrations are provider-side connectors (GitLab, Sentry), not a consumer API.
